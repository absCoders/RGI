Material Requirements Planning

Providing functions used to report and manage the supply and demand status of Inventory Items.

Overview

Supply information consists of the following:

  • On Hand (in Warehouse)
  • On Order (Purchase Orders and Work Orders)
  • Plans (Plans generated automatically by the MRP Update Process)

Demand information consists of the following:

  • (Gross) Shipments Forecast
  • Component Commitments (via Bill of Materials to Make Purchase Orders, Work Orders and Plans)

The Planning Inquiry function provides an excellent view of this Supply and Demand information, in a time-phased grid, showing positions monthly (for 18 months), and in a list of Supply and Demand Events sorted by Date.
