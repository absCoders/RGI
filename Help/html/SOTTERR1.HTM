Territory Codes

This screen is used to maintain records in the Territory Codes table.
Please click here for general help with the operation of any File Maintenance screen.


Territory codes are assigned to entities (ie, warehouses and customers) to provide a geographic placement of the entity, so that inventory and sales information may be reported in the geograpic groupings represented by each territory.

The Division assigned to a Territory is known as the Geographic Division of that Territory, and therefore of the entities (ie warehouses or customers) assigned to that Territory.

FieldDescription (SOTTERR1)
Division Code

Contains the (Geographical) Division containing the territory.

Territory DescriptionContains the description for the territory.
